# Reflection 
This industry talk was quite insightful, and I think it gave me an opportunity to make connections between the concepts I learn in my information systems, as well as data courses, and industry settings. Concepts such as ETLs, data warehouses, Power BI, and cloud infrastructure, which are introduced in class, were quite connected to industry practices at PPG.
I think the major lesson learned from this session was the importance of data integration and documentation. The speaker noted that the data transformation steps are vital and appropriately documented, especially for the maintainability of the system, in case people change roles or leave the company. Further, the discussion on the networking, firewalls, VPNs, regional access restrictions also helped me become more aware of some of the security issues for global companies.
The journey of the speaker from an intern position to a senior position was really motivating. This is because such a journey showed that with learning and experience, an individual can advance in their career in the field of IT and data analysis. This talk has really helped me strengthen my interest in business intelligence and data analysis and has given me the motivation to continue with my learning and acquiring skills relevant to the industry.
