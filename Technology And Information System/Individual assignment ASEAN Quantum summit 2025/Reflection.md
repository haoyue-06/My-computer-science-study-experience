Before attending to ASEAN Quantum Summit 2025, my knowledge about quantum was very minimal. I only had some basics about quantum physics, which I learned when I was in Form 5. I was puzzled as to why I was being sent to the quantum summit, which had absolutely no relevance to my course in data engineering.
My perception was greatly altered after the summit. This was because I realized that quantum technology encompasses not only quantum physics. This is in addition to other areas such as quantum computing, quantum communication, and quantum security. This informed me of the vast areas where quantum technology can apply. These areas consist of data security, artificial intelligence, high-performance computing, and digital infrastructure. This has made me realize the potential of quantum technology in relation to data engineering.
The speakers also stated that the technologies we develop should ultimately be for the good of humanity and improve the life of every person on earth. This really opened my mind on what advanced technologies are all about because it would not only focus on developing but also on the ethics behind it. Moreover, the aspect of talent development and education told me that I and my fellow students are significant in shaping the workforce of the future.
In conclusion, the quantum summit has allowed me to expand my horizon. What I thought was irrelevant to my course is actually an essential learning experience for me. Lessons derived from this summit will remain within my mind and motivate me to further expand my knowledge and foundation of data engineering and remain receptive to any potential new technology concerning quantum in the future.
