# Reflection 
The industry visit to Johor Corporation (JCorp) was also deemed a worthwhile experience and a learning opportunity that improved one's understanding of the application of digital technologies within a large government-linked organization. Exposures were arranged for a better understanding of the organization's digital transformation journey, as well as its application of digital technologies such as cloud computing, data analytics, automation, and artificial intelligence in its business operations.
One of the important insights gained from the visit was an appreciation for the importance of digital transformation to organisational efficiency and flexibility. The explanations provided on JCorp’s cloud-first strategy, data platforms, and AI technology helped connect theoretical concepts of what has been studied in school with what is practiced in industry circles. It has been noted that technology is an integral part of various business processes and not limited to what is handled by the IT department only.
Furthermore, it created awareness about the expectations of the industry and career preparedness. The sharing session also noted that continuous learning, adaptability to emerging technologies, and developing technical and relevant professional skills are important. More appreciation was also cultivated for complementing academic knowledge with practical skills and professional preparedness through continuous learning for competitiveness in the employment market.
This visit to the industry turned out to be really instructive as it effectively closed the gap between theoretical knowledge and professional experience. The experience proved beneficial for the professional awareness developed in the students and gave them a deeper insight into the way technology is applied in modern corporate sectors.
