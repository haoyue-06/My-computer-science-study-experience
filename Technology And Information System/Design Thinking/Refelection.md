The Design Thinking project focused on addressing issues related to cafeteria crowd management and food waste. In this project, I began by collecting data through Google Forms and conducting interviews with students and cafeteria staff to identify the real problems faced by users. Based on the data collected, I analysed the issues and designed a prototype that aims to improve the situation. This structured process helped me understand each stage of problem identification and solution development more clearly.
Through this project, my problem-solving skills were significantly improved. I learned that understanding real user needs is essential before proposing any solution. This experience highlighted that effective outcomes are not determined by technical complexity alone, but by how relevant and usable the solution is for users. In addition, the project allowed me to connect theoretical knowledge with practical application. As a result, I feel more prepared to become an industry-ready graduate who is able to develop solutions that address genuine and meaningful problems.
