# SECR1013 DIGITAL LOGIC
Digital Logic introduces students to the principles of digital logic electronics as the basic for microprocessor-based systems in computers, robots, automobiles and industrial control applications. It covers key topics including number systems and codes, logic gates, Boolean algebra, comibinational logic circuits, latches, flip-flops, counters and shift register, progressing from basic concepts to programmable devices.<br><br>
