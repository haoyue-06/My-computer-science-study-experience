# Digital Logic Lab 1— 17 November 2025

A quiet reminder that every complex system begins with something simple —  
a gate, a switch, a single line of truth in a table.

---

## 📖 Description

This lab session focused on hands-on exploration of digital logic.  
By wiring circuits, testing inputs, and observing LED outputs, abstract logic concepts became tangible and intuitive.

---

## 🔧 Components Used

- **AND Gate**
- **OR Gate**
- **NAND Gate**
- Breadboard & jumper wires
- LEDs for output indication
- Logic inputs (switches or toggle pins)

---

## 🧪 Lab Activities

1. Built individual logic gate circuits on the breadboard  
2. Applied different input combinations  
3. Observed LED output behavior  
4. Checked results against truth tables  
5. Fixed wiring errors and improved connection clarity  

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Simple logic gates form the foundation of complex digital systems  
- Physical circuits reveal how logic behaves in the real world  
- Precision in wiring leads to reliable outputs  
- Every step builds stronger logical thinking and circuit understanding  

---

## 🛠 Skills Practiced

- Circuit construction  
- Logical reasoning  
- Signal tracing & troubleshooting  
- Understanding gate operation  
- Hardware fundamentals  

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

A small lab session — but another important step forward.  
Each LED that lights up is a reminder:  
**Technology begins with logic, and logic begins with understanding the basics.**
