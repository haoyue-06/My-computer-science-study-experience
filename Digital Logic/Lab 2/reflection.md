In Digital Logic Lab 2, I was able to apply the skills of designing digital logic circuits and analyzing the outputs using the DEEDS simulator. By changing the inputs and analysing the outputs, I was able to record the respective outputs in the truth table. Through this, I was able to have a clear understanding of the relationship between the inputs and the outputs of the circuits. It was also possible to interpret the outputs by considering the timing diagram.

Throughout this lab, apart from understanding the implementation of digital logic, I was also able to experience the entire life cycle of a combinational circuit design, right from its formulation to simulation. Another crucial experience gained from this lab is how a circuit, which is made of basic logic gates, is converted into a universal logic gate like NAND or NOR. This has made me understand the importance of the two gates being referred to as universal logic gates because any logical function is possible using these two gates.
