# ULRS1032 Integrity and Anti-Corruption
Integrity is a course that covers the basic concepts of corruption, including the values of integrity, anti corruption principles, forms of corrupt behavior, abuse of power in daily activities and within organizations, as well as methods for preventing corruption.The biggest challenge of Malaysia's government is the corruption. I always hear the news about the corruption cases happened in Malaysia but I think this kind of cases is far away from my life. I did not understand why I need to spend two hours every weeks for this course. However, this course opened my eyes.It changed the way I think about ethics. Now I see ethics in a way both in my daily life and in the job I want to have someday. I learned to make a right choices when facing some tough decisions in real life. I am a student of data engineering. I should be honest in the digital world. I should always keep the information private, safe and secure. I do not use user information that I get in the job for profit. I realize that integrity is not something that just shows up when we work. We need to practise it every day even when we are students. In conclusion, the course taught me that integrity is important and I need to keep working.
